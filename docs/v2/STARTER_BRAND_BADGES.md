## Starter
`/starter`, linked from Discover and own profile. Permanent six-task progression for new and existing accounts. Enrollment when profile is created; existing accounts enroll on first get. Reconstruct known score/session/library and currently completed duel evidence; older duel rounds overwritten by rematch cannot be reconstructed. Future completed duels award immediately before another round can overwrite evidence. Three different Europe/Warsaw dates, not necessarily consecutive; game starts from library and recorded scores/duels count, opening the Hub alone does not.

Tasks 10/20/15/20/15/20 = 100 challenge points. Each task claim grants exactly 20 non-transferable Piksele and points ×10 XP. Max initial currency 120, XP1000. Currency is distinct from points and XP; no spending/shop/exchange in this release. 100 XP per level, starting at 1, starter max level11; level10 badge unlocks at900XP. These are initial product defaults chosen for this implementation, not a previously approved economy design.

Task/reward progress comes from database, never client-sent completion flags. Triggered by score submissions, duel completion, library plays. Clock timing stays client-reported/unverified as in the existing game, so its one-time small reward is not strong anti-cheat. No money, transfer or competitive prize attached. Row lock on account and unique source ledger prevent duplicate claim. No client table grants. Checked narrow definer RPCs with auth.uid, verified profile/suspension and fixed search_path; anon denied private operations. Public profile summary contains no wallet or XP ledger.

Reward thresholds 10 participation marker; 40 frame; 70 banner; 100 completion medal and Dusza imprezy title. Must claim rewards; cosmetics are opt-in switches, preserved permanently. Existing profile banner is not overwritten; cosmetic wrapper can be disabled to reveal the original. All four supplied medal images are visible in own collection; locked medals never appear as earned on public profile. Level10 and starter earned by rules; event/secret through administrator award with rationale, no invented active event or secret. User can pin up to3 earned medals; empty selection shows earned medals automatically. Existing show_badges privacy preference hides medals/level/participation from other viewers. Old text badges remain intact.
